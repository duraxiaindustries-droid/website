About Duraxia Industries

Built on manufacturing excellence.

A new-generation manufacturer powered by decades of group expertise across industrial manufacturing, telecom, and operations.

Who we are

Telecom-grade manufacturing at its best.

Duraxia Industries is a Bangalore-based manufacturer specialised in PLB HDPE Duct and HDPE Pipes for India's rapidly expanding telecom and infrastructure sector.

Our plant in Dabaspet Industrial Village, about 50 km from Bangalore, operates with complete in-house manufacturing and testing facilities — ensuring every product meets the highest standards before it ships.

Visionary leadership

Built by people who know the field.

Duraxia is built on a foundation of expertise across chartered accountancy, operations, marketing, and telecom-sector management.

Our heritage

A legacy of manufacturing trust spanning over a decade.

A decade of manufacturing trust

Duraxia Industries is a proud group company of M/s Sriniwas Industries, located in Harohalli Industrial Area, Kanakapura, Bangalore. Sriniwas has been a trusted name in the Woven Sacks industry for over 10 years, with an annual installed capacity of 3600 MT of Polypropylene Rafia.
